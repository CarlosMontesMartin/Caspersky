# Transparency text for respondents

> Shown on the welcome screen of the form and the kiosk, before the first question.

## English

**Your opinion counts, and it is anonymous.**
This survey takes less than 2 minutes and helps improve how we work in your team.

- We do not ask for your name, email or employee number.
- If you write a name or a phone number in a comment, it is deleted automatically.
- Only results from groups of 5 people or more are shown.
- It is voluntary. Answering or not has no consequences for you.
